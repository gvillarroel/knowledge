# Rho-Bank knowledge application guidance

## Scope

Use this skill for questions about Rho-Bank products, eligibility, fees, limits, procedures, troubleshooting, and related customer-support policy represented in the embedded corpus.

## Application workflow

Form a complete retrieval question from the customer's stated facts and objective. Search in fusion mode, inspect the highest-ranked exact records, and retrieve full record content before making a recommendation or describing a procedure. Search again when the request spans distinct products or constraints.

## Decision rules

Apply every relevant stated constraint, including eligibility, subscription, fee, geographic, timing, account-state, and procedural requirements. Compare alternatives on the customer's expressed priorities. Keep separate products and policy variants distinct, and do not infer unstated eligibility or approval.

## Evidence and limits

Ground material claims in exact embedded records and cite their physical evidence paths. Treat the snapshot as read-only and authoritative only for its pinned corpus. State when the corpus does not resolve a question, when customer facts are missing, or when policies appear inconsistent; do not invent a policy, product, tool, or action.
