## Disposition

Neither rejected admission creates a native job, fitness, semantic miss, retry
or consumed candidate trial. Preserve the failures as unattempted opportunities.
The active controller may finish independent lanes under its existing rules,
but E8 cannot satisfy the all-eight joint gate with these missing baselines.

The maintained organizer has no in-place amendment or unseal operation. Native
external-failure recovery also requires an original source job or trial, absent
for these two admissions. A corrected classifier therefore requires a
prospective study after E8's executable stages are truthfully terminal. Carry
forward all completed E7/E8 evidence, actual attempts, round state and reserved
unavailable profiles. Reuse of unopened private data remains conditional on
fresh independent non-consumption and reservation verification.

The new classifier must exercise both Compose forms, their actual parent/child
relationship, exact phase and task-context restrictions, read-only mount roles,
and negative cases for old entrypoints, whole workspaces and private access.
No process-name workaround or alteration of E8's frozen controls is authorized
by this report. See [ADR 0131](../../../../../.specs/adr/0131-correct-sealed-enterprise-admission-prospectively.md).

The completed [Turso baseline](../turso-baseline-001/README.md) remains valid.
Source-bound offline normalization and aggregate reporting need no admission of
a new live job. They preserve the original completed tree, use fresh output
paths, and enforce ordinary native provenance, completion and metric checks.
Such reporting does not complete E8's downstream publication or private gate.
