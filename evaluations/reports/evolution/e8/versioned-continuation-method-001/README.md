# Versioned continuation: preserve every remaining opportunity

The continuation methodology now preserves both completed search decisions and
two proposals refused before their first native measurement. The independent
reviews support a new archive lineage for the revised package, exact starting
score reproduction and explicit pending-claim accounting. These are prospective
rules; the continuation controller, final historical inventory and execution
admission still require their concrete reviews. No new retrieval score follows
from this preparation.

The fixed Graphify builder changes the complete aggregate package identity.
The [prepared public task versions](../aggregate-public-task-bindings-001/README.md)
also change the native job signatures. Existing jobs retain their actual task,
package and source identities as historical evidence. They cannot be relabeled
as measurements of the revised reference.

| Preserved obligation | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Eight family references and distinct retained profiles | After fixed feasibility qualifies, measure `8 + K` starting cells under the new native profile; determine `K` from terminal history before sealing |
| Historically qualified starting scores | Require exact numeric primary-score reproduction, all qualification gates and native-owner retention agreement before adaptive proposals |
| Full search state | Carry seen profiles, operator/variant positions, misses, rounds, round-start score, retained identities, ordered events and stop reasons; closed catalogs stay closed |
| Turso pending proposal | Preserve `continuation-002`, BM25 `k1=0.6`, as one already claimed variant awaiting its first native measurement |
| Adaptive pending proposal | Preserve `continuation-001`, length normalization `b=0.25` over retained original 014, on the same terms |
| Two unavailable originals | Keep their exclusions, missing fitness and consumed attempt history; they cannot be reissued |

Both pending proposals reached complete realization, then stopped at the
admission guard before native dispatch and before the scheduler observed a
result. Keep their profiles in `seen` and process an explicit pending queue
before the next new proposal in each family. Map the exact full profile and
parent to its versioned package, allocate one first measurement within the
existing variant claim, and observe its outcome once. Preserve preparation
costs and count new native costs when incurred. The original E8 package remains
unexecuted; deleting the claim, ordinary duplicate skipping or a second variant
charge would each lose part of the actual history.

The cumulative ceiling remains **585 proposed variants and five catalog rounds**.
Starting a new native generation zero does not reset it. Necessary feasibility,
reference requalification, joint replay and final gates have separate explicit
measurement allocations and still contribute costs and exposures. Final spent,
reserved and remaining totals must use the terminal inventory, which is not
established by this review.

A mismatch or missing required starting measurement stops this fixed
continuation before further adaptive proposals. Exact reproduction is distinct
from the existing improvement epsilon and joint-replay tolerance. No best-of
choice between old and new scores, replacement profile or semantic rerun can
rescue the gate.

The bounded owner-function probe checked ten public job templates: all ten
task-path changes altered their signatures, all ten skill-list-only controls
preserved them, all ten same-signature comparison controls passed and all ten
mixed-signature comparisons rejected. It created no job, trial, reward or
registration. The methodology review binds 57 sources; the separate pending
review binds 34. The latter verifies the declared predispatch paths and does
not establish global process custody or an execution reservation.

After all remaining searches finish, the required sequence is still all-eight
joint development replay and one frozen merged bundle, paired all-500 retrieval
comparison, then the single whole-bundle private acceptance gate. The declared
distinct-arm allocations are 16, 16 and 32 trials respectively. Private outcomes
cannot drive reselection. All current measurements remain internal retrieval
evidence; this preparation supplies no answer Overall score or public rank.
