## Consequences for the continuation

Two real preparation registries had been omitted from the explicitly supplied
historical study lists. The r1 sibling is also omitted by the prospective
caller's discovery of directories literally named `study`. E6 preparation 002
is already discoverable by that caller's work-root pattern, but still requires
explicit historical and custody accounting. Shared study identifiers cannot
be used to discard either registry. A separately reviewed caller correction is
required before a new execution can be admitted.

Historical public-data reuse remains exposure even when an attempt failed or
produced no score. E6's existing history includes 17 catalog-exhausted decisions,
16 three-consecutive-failure decisions and four duplicate skips; none creates
a new budget. The later E7/E8 counters and permanently unavailable profiles
still require their complete terminal review after the active work settles.

The absence of formal private-release events supports the recorded history; it
does not establish complete exclusive custody or rule out unrecorded disclosure.
The final independent review must recheck every current registry and ledger,
retire dormant plans truthfully, preserve all costs and exclusions, and bind
current custody, the exact caller, actual registration and admission. This
supporting report changes no study state, releases no private data, authorizes
no retry and installs no skill.
