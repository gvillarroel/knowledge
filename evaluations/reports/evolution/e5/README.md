## Fixed comparison

Each complete profile covers 32 dataset-by-family cells: 40 development queries
per dataset, eight families, and every declared retrieval route. EnterpriseRAG
uses 985 documents, Astro 416, architecture books 18, and data-science books 38.
The primary metric is binary nDCG@10 averaged equally over the 32 predeclared
default-route cells. Secondary views report all routes, recall, MRR, reference
coverage, build time, query P95, complete agent/trial time and storage.

Generation zero runs the baseline and four candidates through native Harbor.
One optional later generation may evaluate at most four supported compatible
merges plus the unchanged baseline. Candidate ancestry and complementary cells
must come from the native Pareto archive. The fixed study budget is at most
352 trials, one attempt each, zero retries and zero instruction-model calls.
Prior campaigns and synthetic qualification work are accounted for separately.

Only a qualified final-archive candidate with at least a one-percentage-point
development gain may open the independent validation portfolio. Its exact
package must be frozen first. FiQA and SciFact supply two separate validation
source groups, twelve seeded queries per group and sixteen family cells per
profile. The terminal gate requires at least one percentage point of mean gain,
no regressed cell, complete integrity and no execution error. Its outcome cannot
feed another mutation or selection in this study.

## Reporting and limits

Completed native evidence is published here by generation, dataset, skill,
construction profile and cost/time/accuracy (CTA), including every regression
and incomplete denominator. The overall generation report identifies the best
measured default skill and strongest measured route on each development dataset.
These descriptive maxima do not replace the fixed finalist-selection rule.

Token and provider-charge figures cover the native Harbor trials only.
Assistant-based research, authoring, review and orchestration are outside that
accounting; their development costs are unmeasured. Local CPU execution is also
unpriced. A zero native provider charge does not mean the entire development
effort has zero cost.

Native jobs, model weights, raw datasets, questions, labels, private validation
and detailed traces remain local and ignored. Public reports retain only reviewed
aggregate fields and source commitments. The retrieval measurements do not
establish generated-answer quality, official EnterpriseRAG/BEIR leaderboard
scores, autonomous-agent ability or a universal best skill.
