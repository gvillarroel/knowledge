E10 is **stopped with its starting gate incomplete**. Three native measurements
completed without errors and reproduced their historical primary scores:
Legacy baseline 61.11%, retained Legacy 72.38%, and Embeddings baseline 63.51%
weighted nDCG@10. A demonstrated configuration-serialization defect caused
controller custody refusals. No new variant or skill gain was measured.

review the control prospectively, revalidate compatible native imports without
repeating completed semantic outcomes, and give the remaining families their
declared opportunities.

acceptance gate remain pending. All four E10 stages are stopped and the private
