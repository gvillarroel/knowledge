study design was independently reviewed, sealed and admitted at its running
ledger. Those checks established preparation integrity; they did not establish
that the complete startup control would finish within its limit.

The one complete control measurement then timed out. A static source inventory
finds at least 23,617 direct workspace hash invocations for 7,853 distinct files,

After the timeout, current source-associated Python controls were absent on
Windows and WSL, as were all declared native output paths. This was a bounded
current observation, not a reconstruction of the original process tree. The
maintained organizer recorded the immutable diagnostic and stopped `evaluate`.
The final ledger has seven events, and the old running admission is stale.
