trial errors, one running trial and five pending trials in its last native root

completed generator executions followed by `OSError` errno 5 during Harbor 0.18.0

underlying filesystem cause is unproven. The maintained recovery contract does
not admit these incomplete deterministic jobs without the required recovery
provenance, so no effective jobs or replacement scores were constructed.
