# EnterpriseRAG E9: fixed Graphify builder feasibility

Completed on September 9, 2026. The exact builder candidate qualified in one
native trial, with zero execution errors or retries. Both builds completed and
produced byte-identical output. The primary retrieval result was **8.12% weighted
nDCG@10**, on 120 exposed development questions and 6,000 complete documents.

This evaluation establishes that the revised builder can complete the fixed
workload. Its low retrieval quality remains a target for the separately governed
consultation search. The original E8 builder timed out and has no quality
measurement, so an original-to-revised quality gain or native speed ratio cannot
be calculated.

The [E8 terminal inventory](../e8/terminal-001/README.md) preserves 81 cumulative
variant claims, including two unavailable originals and two pending first
measurements. E9 adds one separately recorded feasibility trial and resets none
of those allocations. The [continuation method](../e8/versioned-continuation-method-001/README.md)
requires twelve fresh starting measurements across eight families before the
remaining searches, followed by joint replay, exact freezing, the paired
all-500 recalculation and one independent acceptance gate. Canonical installation
and that all-family comparison remain incomplete.
