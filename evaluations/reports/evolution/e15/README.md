# EnterpriseRAG E15 continuation

E15 is the separately registered successor to terminal E14. It is limited to
the two families that still lack qualified full-workload measurements: Entity
Graph and Ensemble. The other six family searches remain closed and retain
their published E14 results.

The study has two digest-locked datasets and four planned stages. Its executable
controller, family-scoped reference composition and native owner preflight now
pass. It remains unsealed, has dispatched no native trial, and has not released
validation. Execution requires a new independent curator review, an exclusive
private reservation, the organizer design seal and the development-stage
transition.
