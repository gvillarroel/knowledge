# EnterpriseRAG E15 continuation

E15 is the separately registered successor to terminal E14. It is limited to
the two families that still lack qualified full-workload measurements: Entity
Graph and Ensemble. The other six family searches remain closed and retain
their published E14 results.

The study has two digest-locked datasets and four planned stages. It is
unsealed, has dispatched no native trial, and has not released validation.
Execution requires the remaining controller preflight, a new independent
curator review, an exclusive private reservation and the organizer design seal.
