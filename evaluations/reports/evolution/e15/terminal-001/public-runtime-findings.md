# E15 public runtime review

This is a source review of the prepared, unactivated runtime. It creates no new candidate, native job, model call, retrieval score or private result. E15 activation has separately been rejected by the independent preparation review. Findings below identify work required before a future version can claim complete execution support; they do not authorize further E15 diagnostics.

1. **The prepared runtime does not implement the complete declared lifecycle.** `tmp/enterprise-next-preparation/e15-runtime-001/live_qualification.py` defines `run_family` at line 86 and `main` at line 142 to run only the two reserved first measurements. `controller.py` supplies in-memory family ledger primitives, but there is no executable catalog continuation driver, complete family composition/selection driver, paired joint replay/freeze driver, all-500 driver, or terminal private acceptance driver wired into this runtime. A future runtime must implement and verify that complete sequence before prospective activation. Owner dry-run and doctor results for four job configurations do not demonstrate that orchestration.

2. **The concurrency primitive is local to one process.** `controller.py:260` creates an in-memory `threading.Condition`, active count, consumed set and global-stop field. `live_qualification.py:157` creates a new gate for every launcher. Exclusive-create allocation files help prevent reissue of the same two first-measurement identities; they are useful and should be preserved. They do not constitute a host-wide native-job slot service or count other active jobs. A future complete driver needs a shared, auditable allocation boundary for all allowed native work and must retain consumed identities after crashes. Metadata observations alone do not reserve a slot.

3. **Admission and dispatch are separate operations.** `controller.py:298` performs the current admission callback, then `controller.py:307` persists the allocation, then `controller.py:319` invokes the owner after leaving the local condition. The independent guard is absent from the prepared runtime. A future guard must bind the verified state and source identities through an exclusive allocation lease or equivalent enforced dispatch boundary. Otherwise another process can change the phase, reservation or source between the read-only admission and native use. A signed or digest-bound receipt by itself does not close that interval.

4. **Completeness of source and seal bindings is delegated to a guard that has not been supplied.** `admission.py:81` and `admission.py:87` iterate whichever runtime and source keys the future execution contract contains, without independently requiring the exact frozen inventories. `admission.py:119` binds the sealed protocol to that contract but does not itself check that the organizer's sealed review and baseline are the declared independent review and reference tree. The prospective independent guard must enforce exact path inventories, authoritative implementation digests, the exact registered review, baseline/reference and gate plan, and reject omissions, extras, aliases and digest drift. A future reviewer must test these negative cases against the final frozen runtime. This is an unresolved responsibility, not a claim that a complete independent guard has been bypassed.

5. **Qualification rejection occurs outside the gate's global-stop boundary.** `FirstMeasurementGate.execute` checks only the returned candidate identity before releasing its local active slot. Full `native_score`/qualification validation occurs later in `run_family` through `FamilyLedger.record_qualification`. A returned non-evaluable or unqualified native record can therefore fail after the gate has returned, without setting that gate's stop flag. A second allocation that has not yet been admitted can still pass this local gate. Any future complete dispatcher must latch the declared terminal stop when an unqualified record is observed, while preserving already-admitted in-flight originals and never interpreting such a failure as a quality miss.

An all-500 comparison restricted to the six retained imports would not be an authorized continuation of E15. ADR0162 makes the six family searches terminal and non-rescorable and places the sixteen-trial all-500 comparison after qualification of both open families, all-eight joint reproduction and one complete-bundle freeze. The organizer permits reporting existing jobs without a new evolution study; that statement permits transcription of already observed, appropriately reviewed evidence. It does not authorize bypassing these rejected study gates through a new partial recalculation. Preserve original workload labels and report unfinished gates as unavailable.
