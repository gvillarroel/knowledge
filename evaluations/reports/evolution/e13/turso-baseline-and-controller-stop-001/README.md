## Why the controller stopped

The captured admission observation contained one running container: the
already allocated Turso agent. Its exact current-agent ownership check passed.
There were no recorded conflicting processes or reserved private mounts.
The only flagged mount was the declared read-only pinned public model cache.

Its inherited exception allowed the phases `preparation` and `execution`,
while the current classifier received `development`. The exception was skipped,
so the cache's historical directory ancestor caused a conflict. Adaptive was
denied before its first allocation, and the shared stop prevented further work.
This is a controller declaration error, not evidence that Adaptive or another
retrieval strategy failed its quality test.

The frozen private task tree is unchanged and no private gate was released.
These findings concern the saved source, ledger and observation; they do not
claim to audit unlogged access. The original guard, observation, job and failed
invocation remain preserved. The supported prospective fix is limited to the
specific cache phase declaration and retains exact paths, read-only mode,
current ownership, resource constraints and private-input checks.
