# ADR 0167: Measure the two unadmitted EnterpriseRAG strategies

Date: 2026-09-15.

Status: Accepted for prospective preparation; native admission requires the new study's independent review and seal.

## Context

The ordinary eight-strategy study in [ADR 0166](0166-measure-the-frozen-enterprise-bundle-as-a-separate-public-observation.md)
stopped after an interruption. Four native results qualified. Entity Graph and
Ensemble had environment admission receipts but no final native results;
Graphify and Turso had neither environment admission nor agent execution.
The incomplete native job, eight original allocations, zero-retry budget,
terminal ledger and reviewed partial report remain immutable.

After that outcome was reported, the user instructed work to continue. The
smallest new measurement that does not repeat an executed cell is a prospective
ordinary study of the two previously unadmitted strategy conditions. Missing
results do not qualify the interrupted Entity Graph or Ensemble cells for the
installed external-recovery contract.

## Decision

Register `enterprise-all500-first-executions-001`, with Graphify followed by
Turso in one public dataset and one native job. It contains two new, explicitly
charged original allocations, one attempt each and zero automatic or manual
retries. These are first executions of the two previously unadmitted all-500
task cells, not new independent questions or a resumed original job. Keep the
old eight allocations charged; the two new allocations are additional work.

Preserve the task bytes, full reference bundle, deterministic agent version,
primary routes, scorer, query/corpus membership, skill provisioning, model
cache, isolation policy and resource limits. Change only the declared task
selection, output identities and byte-identical task-copy locations. Preserve
concurrency two, the 10,800-second agent limit, two CPUs and 6 GiB per task.
No candidate may change and no known result may be repeated or replaced.

Use a schema-2 ordinary `evaluation` stage followed by `publication`, both
owned by `harbor-run-results`. Require a substantive independent six-check
review, digest seal and admission receipt before the one-use allocation can
launch. A global source/access failure denies further admission while admitted
siblings settle. Every original counts once, including errors or refusals.

Report the two new native outcomes under their own study identity. A catalog
may place qualified new observations next to the four previously reviewed
observations with explicit study provenance and the common metric contract.
It must retain the two interrupted unavailable rows and must not manufacture
an eight-result native job, claim the original allocation completed, pool the
cohorts as independent evidence, infer causal improvement, or rank runtime as
if concurrency partners were identical.

## Consequences

The new study can fill two measurement gaps without reopening terminal work.
It does not replenish an external-retry cap, retry Entity Graph or Ensemble,
complete E16, select a candidate, release private data or promote a skill.
Those boundaries remain unchanged even if Graphify or Turso score highly.

See the [two-strategy protocol](../../docs/enterprise-all500-first-executions.md)
and [original partial report](../../evaluations/reports/datasets/enterprise-all500-descriptive-001/README.md).
