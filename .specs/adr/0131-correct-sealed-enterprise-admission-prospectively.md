# ADR 0131: Correct sealed Enterprise admission prospectively

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: Accepted for preservation, offline reporting and prospective correction.
Replacement execution requires terminal predecessor stages and a new review.

## Context

During E8's native Graphify baseline, its sealed guard accepted `docker compose`
but rejected that process's `docker-compose` plugin child for the same declared
inherited task context. Entity Graph and Ensemble were refused before native
dispatch. Independent review reproduced the current false positive and found no
competing historical execution or prohibited mount. The original 17 tests did
not exercise the native plugin child. E8's other lanes remain active.

The guard is digest-bound in the reservation, execution contract and sealed
review. The maintained organizer provides no amendment or unseal operation.
Source-bound external-failure recovery requires an original native job or trial;
these two admissions have none. Silently changing the guard would invalidate
the study's frozen execution controls.

## Decision

Preserve E8's sealed controls and original admission failures. Record the
independent diagnostic review through append-only organizer evidence. Let the
existing controller follow its declared independent-lane and stopping rules.
A rejected pre-dispatch opportunity receives no native attempt, fitness,
semantic miss or retry. The missing baselines prevent all-eight completion.

Develop and test a corrected classifier separately from E8's executable inputs.
It must recognize both native Compose process forms while retaining exact
registered task paths, phases, verifier identity, mount destination and read-only
requirements. Test the observed parent/plugin relationship and the existing
negative scope cases. A passing unit test or diagnostic probe is not admission
for replacement execution.

After E8's actual work settles, preserve its complete original inventory and
make its executable stages truthfully terminal. A prospective continuation may
import completed E7/E8 jobs through the native owner with exact candidate,
profile, task and result bindings. Inherit all consumed budgets, seen profiles,
miss counters and rounds. Keep the two interrupted E7 profiles permanently
unavailable. A family with no original native baseline may receive its first
baseline only under the new sealed design.

The independent curator must bind E8 alongside all earlier histories, verify
non-consumption, review the corrected controls and actual native Compose access
probe, and issue a fresh exclusive reservation before new evolution. No formal
replacement registry or dispatch may conflict with E8's active reservation.
The unchanged all-eight joint replay, frozen paired all-500 comparison and
whole-bundle one-way acceptance requirements remain in force.

Permit source-bound offline reports of completed development jobs while live
admission is unavailable. Require native completion, provenance, lock, finite
reward and frozen-parameter checks, a fresh destination and an exact source
commitment. An artifact-only report has no dispatch, model, retry or private
release authority and does not complete downstream study stages.

## Evidence and limitations

The [diagnosis](../../evaluations/reports/evolution/e8/dispatch-control-001/README.md)
preserves the independent review commitments and the limit on historical
process reconstruction. [Turso's original baseline](../../evaluations/reports/evolution/e8/turso-baseline-001/README.md)
is valid partial development evidence. This decision establishes no improvement,
all-eight comparison, private acceptance or permission to install a candidate.
It extends the preservation boundary in [ADR 0130](0130-continue-interrupted-enterprise-search-prospectively.md).
