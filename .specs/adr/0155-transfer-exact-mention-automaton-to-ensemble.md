[construction accounting policy](0137-charge-construction-corrections-within-enterprise-search-caps.md).
It is Ensemble's third nonrefundable proposal within its unchanged cap of 105.

for 104 of 585. Later E14 claims remain additional; this is a dated snapshot.

additional construction debit and preserve every consumed identity and outcome.

Use the sealed Ensemble semantic-return package as parent. Transfer only
`derive_mentions` from the frozen Entity builder into Ensemble's builder copy
of `_entity_graph_model.py`. Preserve every byte outside that function, all
251 other files, the existing memory changes, source-inventory exclusions,

corruption parity in both supported storage layouts before realization. Keep
the new first-measurement identity exclusive and unassigned. Software checks
do not authorize reopening E14 or retrying an earlier native attempt.
