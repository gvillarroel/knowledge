# ADR 0130: Continue interrupted Enterprise searches prospectively

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: Accepted for preservation and prospective continuation preparation.
Replacement execution requires its own reviewed, sealed controls.

## Context

A planned Windows update interrupted E7 during Classical023 and Adaptive019.
The original supervisor was lost and both containers exited. Native completion
and verifier evidence are absent for both jobs. Sixty-six earlier jobs completed;
Legacy and Embeddings have terminal searches, Classical and Adaptive have valid
development incumbents, and four families never started. No candidate was
jointly frozen and no private gate was released.

E7's immutable runner has no subset or restart contract. Native incomplete-job
normalization describes the missing results but does not recover them. The
installed external-failure recovery tool rejects these incomplete roots. Its
specific pre-agent SIGTERM and artifact-collection EIO contracts do not apply.
The independent curator confirmed that an added evolution stage cannot acquire
a downstream private gate inside E7's sealed stage graph.

## Decision

Preserve all original jobs, candidate packages, logs, task roots and frozen
controls. Bind the interruption audit, native incomplete-job report, independent
review and safe aggregate report before making E7 stages terminal as `stopped`.
Do not manufacture completion, rerun either incomplete profile, assign its
fitness or count it as an evaluable miss. Keep private validation unopened.

Prepare a new study prospectively for the remaining authorized opportunities.
Prioritize Entity Graph, Ensemble, Graphify and Turso. Retain the original
finite catalog, construction/consultation separation, strict qualified gain
rule, three consecutive misses and cumulative five-round limit. Import earlier
completed development measurements as historical evidence with exact task,
candidate, lock, result, profile and resource bindings. The native Pareto owner
must revalidate the imports; earlier jobs are not new observations and an E7
archive cannot simply become a previous generation under a different search ID.

Reconstruct each unfinished lane from its complete historical prefix. Reserve
both interrupted complete profiles against any new dispatch. Exclusion neither
changes the incumbent nor increments its miss count. Report incomplete hypothesis
coverage separately from a fully measured plateau. Preserve completed failures
and all prior resource use; a new study identifier does not reset the budget.

The replacement may use the published development runtime audit to declare
longer paired all-500 timeouts before execution. Create new task/runtime versions
for changed limits and preserve E7's original descriptors and adapter. The
original 120-question evidence keeps its original execution contract. Unmeasured
runtime projections are not observed failures or measured speed improvements.

After E7 has no active execution authority, the independent curator must verify
cross-study non-consumption, imported development ancestry and actual executor
isolation. A new exclusive reservation must include terminal E7 and all six
earlier histories. Preserve the old reservation unchanged. Reuse of an unopened
portfolio is conditional on that evidence; any disclosure or consumption
requires fresh private data. Obtain a new review of all replacement tasks,
controls and access probes, register downstream stages, then seal before runs.

Keep the exact all-eight joint replay, one frozen merged bundle, paired all-500
recalculation and whole-bundle one-way acceptance rule from ADR 0129. No private
feedback may drive mutation, ranking or per-family replacement. Installation
requires a separate receipt for the exact accepted bundle.

## Evidence and limitations

The [interruption report](../../evaluations/reports/evolution/e7/interruption-001/README.md)
contains reviewed aggregates and source commitments. The original studies and
native evidence remain ignored. This decision supplies no new fitness, completed
search, final Enterprise ranking, private acceptance or promotion. The preserved
Classical/Luna full-corpus result has a different execution and scoring contract.
