# ADR 0162: Terminate E14 and register E15 before prospective qualification

Status: Accepted for preparation only. E15 execution requires a complete
executable preflight, a new independent curator review, an exclusive private
reservation, a design seal, and current native admission.

Date: 2026-09-12.

## Context

The original E14 controller completed the finite searches for Legacy, Turso,
Adaptive, Embeddings, Classical and Graphify. Entity Graph and Ensemble each
consumed one full-workload starting attempt without producing qualified
retrieval fitness. The controller preserved every outcome and terminated
fail-closed before joint selection, all-500 comparison or private release.

Two separately realized construction candidates subsequently passed their
declared software checks and bounded public runtime diagnostics. Those checks
did not run Harbor, use the complete 6,000-document workload, assign retrieval
fitness, or consume either candidate's reserved first measurement. E14's
organizer ledger nevertheless still described `evolve` as running after the
owner had terminated, so a successor could not truthfully claim exclusive
execution authority.

## Decision

Bind the E14 controller outcome and quiescence evidence in one development-stop
decision, record that decision on the running E14 evolution stage, and stop all
four E14 stages. The downstream comparison and publication stages are stopped
as unexecuted. Validation is stopped sealed and unreleased. The resulting
14-event ledger is terminal, has no next action, and cannot be restarted or
amended into a new evolution campaign.

Create a new empty organizer registry named `enterprise-e15`. Register the exact
16-task development source used by E14 and the exact 16-task private source that
E14 never released. Plan `evolve`, `recalculate`, `validate`, and `publish` in
that order. Registration mechanically digest-locks the inputs; it does not by
itself establish that the private source remains unseen, independent, suitable,
or exclusively reserved. A new independent curator must verify those properties
and the complete E15 design before sealing. If that review cannot establish the
private source's eligibility, E15 cannot run; a replacement study must register
a fresh private cohort rather than changing E15 after sealing or execution.

Keep the six completed E14 family searches terminal and non-rescorable. The new
primary controller, `harbor-reflective-pareto-search`, may import their exact
completed development evidence only after validating original task, candidate,
lock, result, profile, resource and terminal-state bindings. Closed family
allowances remain nontransferable. E15 may schedule new development work only
for Entity Graph and Ensemble.

Map `entity-bounded-selection-001-first-measurement` and
`ensemble-mention-automaton-transfer-001-first-measurement` to their exact sealed
candidate trees. Each identity has cardinality one, one attempt and zero
automatic retries. Their construction proposal charges were already paid and
must not be charged again. The failed E14 starting identities remain consumed
and cannot be renamed, retried, or used as fallback measurements.

A successful first measurement qualifies its candidate as that family's
prospective continuation reference. It does not establish a gain against the
failed E14 starting attempt. Only then may the family continue its remaining
finite construction catalog with the inherited three-consecutive-evaluable-miss
rule and five-round ceiling. A failed or unavailable first measurement stops
that family and therefore prevents the whole-bundle gate.

The cumulative proposal ledger starts E15 at 140 of 585 claims. Six closed
families retain 272 unused, nontransferable slots. Entity Graph retains 71 of 80
and Ensemble retains 102 of 105 possible slots, for at most 173 further open
family claims. The cumulative development execution ledger preserves 61
consumed allocations, including the two failed E14 starts and the original E13
Turso import. Proposal charges and native executions remain separate measures.

Preserve the established runtime: 120 development questions, 112
retrieval-eligible questions, 6,000 complete documents, population weight 470,
two CPU threads and 6 GiB per agent, 3,600 seconds per agent, the 2,400-second
builder subprocess limit, two CPU threads, 2 GiB and 180 seconds per verifier,
one attempt, zero retries, no network, no model calls, and at most two native
jobs globally. A runtime or workload change requires a separately versioned
contract and cannot consume either reserved first-measurement identity.

After both open families finish, compose the qualification references and the
eight retained family profiles separately. The reference composition includes
the two newly qualified construction packages without crediting them as
retrieval gains; the selected composition adds only subsequent development
selection. Require the 16-trial paired joint development replay to reproduce all
family scores before freezing one whole bundle. Then run the 16-trial paired
all-500 comparison without reselection. Release the 32-trial private acceptance
only once for that exact frozen bundle. Every family must qualify and have a
nonnegative paired mean across both private source groups. Private feedback can
neither reopen E15 evolution nor replace a family.

Do not transition E15 evolution to running until the executable controller and
composition logic pass focused and native dry-run preflight, the registration
and candidate bindings still verify, the new independent review covers every
locked task and control, the exclusive reservation passes, and the organizer
design seal verifies. Registration, software diagnostics and this decision grant
no native execution authority.
