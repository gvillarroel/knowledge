# ADR 0134: Requalify versioned Enterprise profiles without resetting search

Date: 2026-09-09

Status: Accepted for prospective preparation. Execution requires the exact
reviewed controller, terminal history, registered design and current admission.

## Context

[ADR 0132](0132-qualify-graphify-builder-before-consultation.md) separates the
fixed Graphify builder feasibility trial from later consultation evolution.
[ADR 0133](0133-version-exact-builder-bindings-in-feasibility-tasks.md) requires
explicitly versioned task bindings for the changed aggregate package.

The maintained Pareto owner binds task paths, native task identities, runtime
settings and the complete baseline package. The prepared public task versions
change the declared job signatures. Existing E7/E8 jobs cannot become qualifying
measurements of those new tasks and packages through a renamed path or candidate.
Historical search decisions still need to determine which opportunities remain.

## Decision

After fixed Graphify feasibility qualifies and predecessors become terminal,
start a new native archive lineage for the versioned reference. Keep the
original jobs, archives, packages, scores and ancestry as immutable historical
evidence. They do not become current archive fitness or a previous generation
of a different native profile.

Measure eight versioned family references and each distinct retained-profile
transplant. The starting allocation is `8 + K` native trials, with `K` and the
exact packages fixed from terminal history before the new study seal. A profile
identical to the reference needs no second alias measurement. The separate
Graphify feasibility task has a different declared path from the prepared
aggregate task, so its job cannot replace that archive's baseline measurement.

For every starting role with a qualified historical measurement, require exact
numeric reproduction of its bound native primary fitness, unchanged required
qualification and native-owner retention agreement. Requalification tolerance
is zero. This is a distinct rule from the existing `1e-12` strict-improvement
epsilon and joint-replay tolerance. An absent, unqualified or mismatched required
result stops this fixed continuation path before new adaptive proposals. Never
choose the better of the two observations, substitute an old score, replace the
retained profile or rerun a semantic result to satisfy this gate.

Preserve the complete historical scheduler state: all seen profiles, pending
proposals, permanent unavailable originals, operator and variant positions,
misses, round, round-start score, retained profile and identity, stop reason and
ordered events. Map historical IDs explicitly to the newly measured package
records. A new native generation zero is not a new campaign round. Requalification
does not reopen a closed catalog or count as a successful mutation.

Keep verified predispatch proposals in `seen` with an explicit pending
first-measurement disposition. Turso `continuation-002` and Adaptive
`continuation-001` were claimed and realized before their guards refused
dispatch; neither reached the native owner or the scheduler's observation.
Restore their post-claim, pre-observation state and complete each obligation
before requesting another new proposal in that family. Preserve the already
advanced cursor, exact full profile, original parent and missing observation.
Do not delete the claim, claim it again, recompute it from a new incumbent, add
a semantic miss or skip it as an ordinary duplicate.

After the required requalification and admission, map each pending claim to
one first native measurement of its exact versioned package. Bind original and
new identities and require an exclusive claim-to-attempt mapping. Observe its
accepted native outcome exactly once, then continue the existing cursor. The
original E8 package remains unexecuted. The final inventory must revalidate
the absence of an original dispatch; ambiguity stops the mapping for review.
A further admission refusal grants no automatic resubmission loop.

Each pending claim already occupies one cumulative variant slot. List its
reserved first measurement inside that existing claim and account for actual
new execution costs when incurred. Neither a new package realization nor its
native measurement creates a second variant slot or cancels the first charge.
These are not reference/retained requalification trials. Permanently unavailable
originals that already started remain excluded before any pending mapping;
renaming a candidate or changing its package digest cannot override that rule.

The cumulative limit remains five catalog rounds and 585 proposed variants,
with inherited per-family limits. Determine consumed, reserved and remaining
allowances from the final history; never reuse E8's initial remaining-count
constant. Separately allocate and account for feasibility, requalification,
joint replay, final comparison and private acceptance. Their costs and exposures
are real and do not replenish the variant budget.

After all family searches finish, realize and replay the exact merged bundle
against the versioned reference across all eight families: 16 development
trials for distinct arms. Require score reproduction and all native gates,
then freeze the whole bundle. Run the paired all-500 comparison with 16 trials
and no reselection. The one-way private acceptance remains 32 trials across
two source groups, eight families and both arms; every required cell qualifies
and each family's paired mean gain must be nonnegative. Whole-bundle rejection
cannot be rescued by replacing one family after private feedback.

## Evidence and consequences

The independent methodology assessment binds 57 sources and recommends the
exact-reproduction rule adopted here. Its organizer directory digest is
`eda7041e89807e7024266c919d412dbd072e65435c1adccf16aa30caafbb2e42`.
The bounded public probe changed only task paths in ten in-memory native job
configurations; all ten signatures changed and all ten synthetic mixed-profile
comparisons were rejected. It created no native job or new fitness.

The separate pending-proposal assessment binds 34 sources and establishes the
two exact predispatch boundaries. Its organizer directory digest is
`345719d0b8c334ea54a695ce38aea0c0e20d8619c9d55b83872a349cc94cb1d3`.
The [reviewed aggregate](../../evaluations/reports/evolution/e8/versioned-continuation-method-001/README.md)
preserves both review commitments and the bounded probe's scope.

The implementation must separately prove state mapping, pending-proposal
accounting, exact registered datasets and package identities, exclusive private
custody, the final study seal and current execution admission. The fixed
feasibility protocol and caller remain unchanged. This decision establishes no
current-version score, native speedup, final selection, acceptance or installation.
