# Continue Enterprise searches by family readiness

Date: 2026-09-11

Status: Accepted for prospective preparation. A new registered design, exact
native identities and current independent custody admission are required before
execution. Existing studies and their stopped gates remain unchanged.

## Context

[ADR 0134](0134-requalify-versioned-enterprise-profiles-without-resetting-search.md)
requires all starting roles to qualify before its fixed continuation can issue
new proposals. Entity Graph and Ensemble did not qualify, so that path stopped.
Turso, Adaptive and Graphify still have declared development opportunities.
Changing the old gate after observing those failures would change its method.

The [evidence-index candidate](../../evaluations/reports/evolution/e11/entity-evidence-index-reuse-001/README.md)
provides a new complete reference with software checks and an unassigned native
first measurement. Independent public-source review supports a new study whose
readiness is determined by each family's own starting results. This does not
establish feasibility or a retrieval gain for the candidate.

## Decision

Use complete package
`sha256:c4d3c66cb4567ac4bbb6236e183c17bccaad9f4cccac77f67a382cfdbc735faf`
as the prospective common reference. Freeze all eight reference roles and four
distinct retained-profile transplants: Legacy, Classical, Adaptive and Turso.
Measure all twelve under the new bound native profile. Do not import old jobs
as fitness for changed task or package identities. Ten previously qualified
roles must reproduce their historical primary score exactly, including Graphify.
Entity and Ensemble have no qualified historical target; execution errors are
not zero-score targets.

A family may continue only after every one of its starting roles qualifies,
reproduces any required historical score and agrees with native-owner retention.
A missing, unqualified or mismatched outcome stops that family without retry.
Other ready families may continue. Source integrity, accounting, custody and
global resource failures stop all dependent work. No family may repair the
frozen reference in place.

Use the maintained native Pareto owner for measurement and selection, with one
development stage and a common whole-bundle selection ancestor. Register the
disjoint development and independent validation portfolio, and plan the final
gate before evolution begins. The curator must independently verify the
unopened portfolio's commitments, exposure history, isolation and suitability;
this public-source review does not establish freshness or grant private access.

Freeze a finite queue in this order: Turso, Adaptive, Graphify, Legacy,
Classical, Embeddings, Entity Graph and Ensemble. Give each family its initial
turn before requeuing another step. A subsequent step contains at most one
candidate proposal and its one observation. Preserve sequential order within
each family, with at most two native jobs globally and each job's existing
resources, attempt count and zero-retry policy. Completion time may change
inter-family order; simultaneous completions use the declared family order.
The native allocation boundary must check a shared global stop/admission state
before every job, including successive starting roles inside one callback.
The thread-pool limit alone does not enforce nested native concurrency.

Preserve the full historical scheduler: profiles seen, parents, cursors, misses,
rounds, retained identity, unavailable originals and recorded closed catalogs.
Complete the already charged Turso `k1=0.6` and Adaptive `b=0.25` first
measurements before any new proposal in their families. Never re-claim them,
recompute them from a new incumbent or retry a consumed outcome.

The current allowance is 91 of 585: 81 historical catalog claims and ten
construction corrections, including eight Entity and two Ensemble corrections.
Construction charges reduce the same family cap but do not invent profile
identities or semantic misses. Five open families have at most 373 further
claims; the 121 unused closed-family slots remain nontransferable. Keep the
five-round ceiling and three consecutive unique evaluable non-improvements per
mechanism. Native execution costs and first measurements remain distinct from
proposal charges.

The sole unassigned `entity-evidence-index-reuse-001-first-measurement` may map
exclusively to the new Entity generation-zero reference role. Bind its original
reservation, new study/role, exact complete candidate, unchanged full workload,
task/runtime identities and one attempt before allocation. Count one outcome,
one execution cost and no additional proposal. It occupies one of the twelve
starting roles. Do not also allocate an ordinary qualification or use an
ordinary fallback after a consumed failure. A changed or lighter workload cannot
use this substitution.

Final completion still requires all eight qualified references and completed
search obligations, the exact composed reference/candidate and 16-trial paired
development replay with reproduction and native gates, one frozen whole bundle,
the 16-trial paired all-500 comparison without reselection, and the 32-trial
independent gate across both source groups, all eight families and both arms.
Every required cell must qualify; every family's paired mean gain must be
nonnegative. A failed or missing family prevents whole-bundle acceptance.
Private feedback cannot guide another family selection, repair or partial
promotion.
